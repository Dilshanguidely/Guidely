FAQs

Questions? Start here.

A few helpful answers for students beginning their career journey.

What is Guidely?

Guidely is a student-focused career guidance website concept designed to help learners explore careers, courses and educational pathways.

Do I need to know my career goal already?

No. Exploring different options is a useful first step. You can learn about fields before deciding what suits you.

Can I book a mentor through this website?

Not yet. This version is a front-end prototype. Mentor registration, verification, scheduling, payments and booking are not currently available.

Is the information a guarantee of admission or success?

No. Always verify exam dates, eligibility, fees and admission requirements through the relevant official institution or exam website.

How can younger students stay safe when seeking guidance?

Involve a parent or trusted adult before arranging contact with an unfamiliar mentor. A future Guidely mentoring service should include mentor verification, privacy protections and guardian consent where appropriate.
